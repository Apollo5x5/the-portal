How to get your Google Calendar secret iCal link:
  1. Open Google Calendar on your browser.
  2. Click the 3 dots next to your calendar on the left > Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll to "Integrate calendar" and copy "Secret address in iCal format".
  4. Paste above and click Save & Apply Sync.
